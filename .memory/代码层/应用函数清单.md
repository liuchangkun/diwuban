### 1.2 连接租约函数（3个）

**路径**：`app/adapters/db/connection_lease.py`
**签名**：`lease_connection(timeout=30.0, lease_duration=300.0) -> ConnectionLease`
**用途**：租用数据库连接（带自动续租）

        cur.execute("SELECT * FROM fact_measurements WHERE ...")
